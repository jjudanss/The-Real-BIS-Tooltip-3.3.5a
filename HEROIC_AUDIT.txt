THE REAL BIS-TOOLTIP 3.3.5a — HEROIC DIFFICULTY AUDIT
Bundled with the first release
Authoritative BiS workbook: BiS_List_by_Kama_Corrected-v2.xlsx

SCOPE
- 10 class worksheets and 37 build columns.
- 627 filled equipment cells; alternatives split by slash, plus sign, and line break produce 647 equipment candidate occurrences.
- 210 distinct normalized workbook lookup names resolve to 205 distinct item IDs (aliases and repeated names account for the difference).
- Every lookup name is listed in HEROIC_AUDIT.csv with its item ID, audit class, note, profile use, and database link.

RESULT
- 178 distinct IDs are mapped to Heroic raid variants (this includes Heroic 10-player items; Heroic does not mean only 25-player Heroic).
- 27 distinct IDs are retained as non-Heroic-mode/source-system exceptions because there is no same-name Heroic raid counterpart in the 3.3.5a item data. These are not automatically wrong BiS choices.
- Seven verified Normal-to-Heroic item-ID corrections are included in the bundled item catalogue.

7. Dreamhunter's Carbine: 51561 -> 51834 (Heroic ICC 10-player)

NON-HEROIC-MODE EXCEPTIONS
The CSV provides the individual row note. The exception families include:
- Vendor/relic items purchased with Emblems (librams, idols, sigils, totems, Herkuml War Token, Sentinel's Winter Cloak, Verdigris Chain Belt).
- Ashen Verdict reputation rings (progression by reputation, not raid difficulty).
- Crafted item: Pillars of Might.
- PvP item: Wrathful Gladiator's Greatstaff.
- Legendary items: Val'anyr and Shadowmourne.
- Ulduar items that use the hard-mode system rather than a separate Heroic difficulty variant: Meteorite Crystal and Libram of the Sacred Shield.
- Signified Ring of Binding (the workbook's display spelling is "Signet Ring of Binding"), listed as Onyxia 25-player Normal-only; Onyxia has no Heroic mode.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This audit checks same-item Heroic difficulty equivalents and the correctness of the bundled item IDs. It does not re-simulate stats or re-theorycraft the overall BiS ranking of every build. Where an item has no same-name Heroic mode version, the workbook recommendation is preserved instead of substituting an unrelated item. Private realms may use custom item templates/IDs; compare the item link and tooltip in your realm if its database differs from standard Wrath 3.3.5a.
